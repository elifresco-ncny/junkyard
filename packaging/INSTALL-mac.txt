JUNKYARD - Mac (macOS 11 or newer, Apple Silicon and Intel)

The easiest way is the .pkg installer. To install from this zip instead:
1. Copy Junkyard.vst3 into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
   Copy Junkyard.component into /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
2. Restart your DAW.

To uninstall, delete those two files.
